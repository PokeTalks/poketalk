🗺️ How to Play

1. Roll the Poké Ball to move.
2. Land on a green tile to climb a ladder.
3. Red tiles send you down a villain's trap.
4. You must reach tile 100 exactly to win.
5. If your roll goes past 100, you stay put.
6. Trainers take turns automatically.
